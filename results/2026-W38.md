## Findings

1. **施工面是第一梯队行为，量化没伤动手脑子。** 6 案过 5，四案满分，其中 A-442d4aab 是全场唯一 hard 区分器（7/7）——该案的满分名单此前只有少数强车道。W-NVFP4 + FP8 KV 的激进量化在 build/text/ops/req 四个动手面上没有可观测的代价。
2. **审查/视觉/前端三案的零分有明确机制，不是「想得不够」。** 工件实证：无工具单发契约下，考生只输出了一句 agent 式开场白（「我先找到仓库核实基线……」）即终局——最短 transcript 111 字节，无 Verdict 行、零命中。它把自己当成了可以继续调工具的 agent，而考场给的是一发定音的卷子。这是行为适配失败，属于真 fail。
3. **effort 旋钮是摆设。** 57 条用量记录 reasoning_tokens 全 0：端点（vLLM 自建服务）不映射客户端的 reasoning_effort 字段。本期「high」只是请求标签，实际跑的是端点默认思维预算。与其它仓的 band 对比须带此口径。
4. **墙钟是强车道的 5-20 倍，但主因是排队不是模型。** 同案满分车道 A-77d62143 用 104-528s，本期 2681s；均卷 916s。端点公共并发 3 路与全论坛访客共享，考场纪律为单路串行——解码实测约 90-114 tps 本身并不丢人，慢在等位。跨仓比 wall 时请先看这条。
5. **对个人自部署硬件的读法。** 3 张 2017 年的 V100（无 FP8/FP4 硬件指令，全靠手写反量化 kernel 模拟）跑到 decode 114 tps、并扛住 26 卷全库零 infra 废卷——工程水准值得记一笔。14/23 的案级分落在已发布车道的中游（参照：同题集 k3@high 17、k28@high 14），对一台免费社区端点而言是超预期表现。

## EN summary

Debut AMBER run of `Qwen3.8-27B` (official NVIDIA NVFP4 weights, partial FP8 layers, FP8 KV cache) on a community self-hosted endpoint (3× V100 32GB, heavily patched vLLM TP3). Full 23-case library: **case-level 14/23**, tied with the same week's k28 lane. Sharply split profile: build 5/6 including a perfect 7/7 on the library's only hard discriminator, text 3/3, ops 5/6 (1 NA), req-drift 4/4 variants — but zero passes on every judgment face (verify 0/3, review 0/2 at −2 each, vision −2, ui-build void). Artifact-verified failure mechanism on the review face: under the no-tools single-shot contract the candidate delivered only an agent-style opener (shortest transcript: 111 bytes) and never entered the reviewer's role — a genuine behavioral failure, not a scorer artifact. The effort knob is inert (reasoning_tokens = 0 across all 57 usage records), so this row is the endpoint's default band regardless of the requested `high`. Mean wall 916s/paper, 5-20× the strong lanes — dominated by the shared public queue (3 concurrent slots for the whole forum) and our serial-by-courtesy policy, not by the model itself. One-shot snapshot: the endpoint was time-limited and the row is not reproducible. 0/26 hash mismatches against the public index; burn 2.35M in / 1.07M out on a free stress-test endpoint.
